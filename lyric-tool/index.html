Waveform: click an empty spot to play from there; drag a vertical line to change its time — the others stay put until it reaches them, then it pushes them along ahead of it; scroll to zoom. Thin lines = 16th notes, bright lines = beats, thick lines = bars. The short ticks along the bottom show vocal onset strength.
Scrolling sideways: drag the bright box in the whole-song strip under the waveform (click outside the box and it jumps there); or hold an empty spot of the waveform and drag left / right; or use a horizontal wheel / Shift+wheel; Shift+←→ scrolls half a screen. When the playhead runs off the right edge the view pages forward by itself. The small bars at the bottom of the strip are the lines (green = confirmed, pink = current line).
Latency calibration
The song plays at 1× from the drums of the intro. Press J on every beat (quarter notes); after 16 presses your key latency (headphone / audio-device delay + reaction time) is worked out, and from then on it is subtracted from every tap.
